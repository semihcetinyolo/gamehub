# Cartograph level work

For creating, replacing, reordering or repairing levels, apply the `cartograph-levels` skill at `/Users/semihcetin/.config/claiface/codex/skills/cartograph-levels/SKILL.md` and read `tools/stained/README.md`.

For generating, replacing or completing stained-glass (vitray) artwork, apply `/Users/semihcetin/.config/claiface/codex/skills/cartograph-vitray/SKILL.md`. Use exact final guides, validate pane colours, and explicitly connect imported assets to the game catalogue.

All active levels must pass `tools/stained/audit_geometry.py --stamp` before `build_levels.js`. Preserve authored curves and symmetry where shared borders are clear; sharpness alone is not a defect. Use the v4 audit: actual shared borders, readable separated T junctions (no ambiguous four-or-more-way contacts), visibly connected independently paintable panes at normal scale including 3D seams, definite logical starting moves and matching final artwork. Hairline bridges hidden beneath lead must not join visually separate pieces into one fill region. Prefer moving background dividers over bending the subject, and reuse existing glass textures for geometry refreshes when suitable. `readable_junctions.py` is the approved production junction pass; the user approved the full rollout after reviewing eight levels.

Meaningful subject contours are immutable constraints. Resolve contacts by moving/removing background divisions or adjusting whole source parts and spacing. Do not use short-edge collapse, gap warping, or an unconstrained fan fallback to dent the subject. Restore older damage from authored curves; proximity alone must not classify a parallel background line as a subject. Keep pristine source arcs locked during restoration. Leave prominent subject panes unpainted when the deduction rules permit; prepare `clueFocus` metadata after final geometry/solution and preserve the cat tutorial.

Judge contacts on what the player sees, not on polygons: `tools/stained/visible_contacts.py` (lead-radius morphological opening; >= 4 px visible contact, >= 5.5 px visible gap, one visible piece per pane, >= 100 px² panes; large scenes at 2x). Background partitions are provisional and never bend or bump the level's subject; tiny atoms go to background neighbours (`repair_visible_contacts.py`, `rebuild_authored.py`). After every build run `tools/stained/build_v1_levels.py` for the in-game V1/V2 version switch.
