## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: portrait background environment for a cozy mobile cafe puzzle game.
Primary request: A beautifully crafted 3D miniature cafe interior, softly isometric elevated view, tactile handmade clay and painted wood, sophisticated casual game art.
Scene/backdrop: A small sunlit coffee shop with cream plaster walls, sage green paneling, pale oak cabinetry, a curved arched window at upper left with warm sunlight, a tiny espresso machine and stacked ceramic cups on a back counter in the upper third, leafy plants at the outer edges, muted terracotta details. Lower two thirds is very quiet pale cream and warm beige tiled floor, mostly empty negative space for an overlaid playable board.
Composition/framing: Portrait 2:3 composition. Environment details concentrated at the top and extreme side edges. The central lower area must be calm, bright, low contrast and uncluttered. Slightly elevated orthographic camera, real depth, rounded beveled edges, soft contact shadows, premium 3D mobile game render.
Lighting/mood: Warm morning light, soft ambient occlusion, welcoming and relaxed.
Constraints: No words, no letters, no interface, no characters, no watermark. No overlaid UI or game pieces. Not flat vector art.

### Kitchen generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: portrait environment background for a premium cozy casual mobile food-sorting game.
Primary request: a warm inviting miniature country kitchen rendered as high-quality rounded 3D game art, suitable for a calm enjoyable mobile puzzle game for adult players.
Composition: portrait 2:3. A soft apricot plaster wall and creamy tiles occupy the top half. A coral and ivory striped fabric valance only at the very top. The middle must be empty and low contrast because interactive objects overlay it. Bottom 55 percent is a broad empty honey-colored wooden kitchen countertop seen from a slightly elevated orthographic angle, softly lit and completely clear. Style: tactile toy-like 3D, beautifully rounded chunky shapes, soft painted materials, warm sunlit colors, golden oak, apricot peach, sage, creamy ivory, gentle ambient occlusion, soft contact shadows. Mobile game environment, not photographic, not an interface mockup.
